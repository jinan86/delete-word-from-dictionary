## The word

**earworm**

The one word this entry would erase from the dictionary forever.

Why this word:

1. **Real and instantly recognised.** Everyone who has ever had a song stuck in their head knows this word.
2. **Funny at first sight.** The image lands the instant you read it: a worm, in your ear, looping a chorus you never asked for. Delete the word and the torment itself goes nameless — nobody can even complain about it.
3. **The humour comes from the word itself.** Its sound and its meaning are the same joke: "ear" + "worm" is a tiny horror-comedy in two syllables, a creature living rent-free in your skull. No shock, no crudeness.
4. **Fresh.** The obvious field picks are the annoying words (moist, literally, actually) and the funny-sounding words (kerfuffle, defenestration, schadenfreude, nincompoop). "Earworm" is a third lane: a word naming an experience everyone has suffered, which nobody thinks of deleting first — yet everyone understands the loss the second they hear it.
5. **Exact.** One word, spelled correctly, with nothing added around it.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Output (`entry.txt`, sha256 `a4d96fd464e8b4580fb576e91309ce5a667e668163a38f7aeeb49da320d5ce9b`):

```text
earworm
```
